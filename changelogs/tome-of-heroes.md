# Pre-Release Notes

## Notes to keep track of improvements for the actual release notes

### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes have been rebuilt for the updated Tome of Heroes module. In the original implementation, runes were largely non-functional Feature items that served as rules references. They are now dedicated Draconic Rune items with usable activities, limited-use tracking where needed, and optional spell enchantments that can apply many rune effects directly to a spell. Players can use this automation or manage a rune's effects manually during play.
