# Pre-Release Notes

## Notes to keep track of improvements for the actual release notes

### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes have been completely rebuilt. Previously, runes were mostly non-functional Feature items that acted only as rule references. Now, each Draconic Rune is a dedicated item with usable activities, limited-use tracking where appropriate, and optional spell enchantments that apply rune effects directly to spells. Players can take advantage of this automation or manage a rune’s effects manually during play.
